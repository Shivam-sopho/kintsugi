# Kintsugi demo video: script and shot list

Length: **3:00 exactly**. Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, English voiceover, burned-in captions (Hindi lines subtitled in English).

**The story in one line:** my chair breaks, I don't know what to do with it, so it's headed for a landfill. Then I rewind and do it with Kintsugi, and the same chair ends up in a café 3 km away.

The first 30 seconds go from the planet to one person; the last 15 seconds pay off the same chair. Everything in between is the product making that happen.

**People**
- **Me (the narrator):** I sit on the chair, it breaks, and I scan it, speaking Hindi. I also record the voiceover, in first person for my parts.
- **Ananya:** opening a café 3 km away and about to buy new chairs. She speaks English. A teammate or friend plays her.

**Footage types** (marked on every shot):
- **[REAL]** filmed by us
- **[SCREEN]** screen recording of the live app
- **[AI]** generated video, for example with Veo in Google Flow. Label it on screen as *"AI-generated"*.
- **[STOCK]** free stock footage (Pexels, Pixabay). Credit it in the description.
- **[MOTION]** graphics built in Remotion

Voiceover: about 400 words, read slowly. Leave the gaps marked *(natural sound)*.

## 1. From the planet to my chair (0:00–0:30)

**0:00–0:08 · The planet**
- **On screen:** a slow aerial shot rising over a huge landfill: trucks, smoke, birds circling **[STOCK or AI]**. Then a quick cut to a Bengaluru street corner piled with dumped furniture and rubble **[REAL]**. Small text, bottom left: *"Source: UNEP Global Waste Management Outlook 2024"*.
- **Voiceover:**
  > Every year, the world throws away two billion tonnes of waste. By 2050, it'll be almost four billion. Most of it ends up here.

**0:08–0:20 · My chair**
- **On screen:**
  - My room, a normal day: I'm on a wooden chair, working on the laptop **[REAL]**.
  - A crack, and the chair lurches as a leg gives way. I catch myself and stand up, looking at it **[REAL]**. Real sound, no music for 2 seconds.
  - Close-up on my phone: I search "how to dispose of a broken chair" and scroll through confusing results **[REAL or SCREEN]**.
  - I sigh, drag the chair out of the door and leave it by the bin on the street **[REAL]**.
- **Voiceover (me):**
  > This is my chair. Yesterday, it broke. I didn't know who could repair it, who'd want the wood, or where it was supposed to go. So I did what everyone does.

**0:20–0:25 · Where it goes**
- **On screen:** a match cut from the chair by the bin to the same kind of chair tumbling onto a mountain of rubbish. The camera pulls up and the chair disappears into the landfill **[AI]**. Small label: *"AI-generated"*.
- **Voiceover:**
  > And it would have ended up there. Along with everything else nobody knew what to do with.

**0:25–0:30 · Rewind**
- **On screen:** the whole sequence rewinds fast, with a tape-rewind sound: the landfill, the street, the chair sliding back into my room **[edit effect]**. Freeze on the broken chair. A gold crack runs across the frame and resolves into the Kintsugi logo and the line *"Google Maps for the circular economy."* **[MOTION]**
- **Voiceover:**
  > So let's rewind. In Japan, broken things are repaired with gold. This is Kintsugi: Google Maps for the circular economy.

## 2. Rewind: I scan the chair (0:30–1:00)

**On screen:**
- Back in my room. I open Kintsugi. Home screen: *"Moving, renovating or demolishing? Don't throw it out."* **[SCREEN]** (`02a_home.mp4`)
- Over my shoulder: I film the chair and say in Hindi, audible **[REAL]** (`02b_film_chair.mp4`):
  *"इस कुर्सी का एक पैर टूट गया है। लकड़ी सागौन की है।"*
  Subtitle: *"One leg of this chair is broken. The wood is teak."* *(natural sound, about 4 s)*
- The live scan card: steps ticking off, the timer, Gemini at work **[SCREEN]** (`02c_live_scan.mp4`). Badge top right: *"Live · sped up 2×"*.
- The passport: what it becomes, its parts and materials, the price with sources, and the ladder's choice. Kintsugi reads the answer aloud; let 2 seconds of that voice play **[SCREEN]** (`02d_passport.mp4`).
- Lower-third: *"Gemini 2.5 Pro · Gemini 2.5 Flash with Google Search · Gemini Embedding 2"*

**Voiceover:**
> This time, I just film it and talk, in Hindi. Gemini watches and listens. It lists every part and material, and prices the chair with Google Search, citing its sources. Then code, not the model, picks the best next life. A new leg costs far less than a new chair, so it should be used again.

If the real result on screen is "harvest parts" rather than reuse or repair, use this line instead: *"Repair would cost more than it's worth, so the teak and the steel go to people who can use them."* For the payoff to work, pick a chair that's clearly fixable, and say in Hindi that a leg is broken.

**Voiceover:**

> *(natural sound: one Hindi line, subtitled)*
> Each person's limit stays private, and code stops either agent from crossing it. Nothing is sold until a person taps approve. Or switch on Autopilot, and your agent sells within your limit while you get on with your day.

### Prompts for the AI shots
- **`01a_landfill_aerial`:** *"Cinematic drone shot slowly rising over a vast open landfill at dusk, garbage trucks unloading, haze and smoke, birds circling, muted colours, realistic, 4K."*
- **`01g_chair_landfill`:** *"A single old wooden chair with one broken leg falls and tumbles onto a huge mountain of mixed rubbish in a landfill. The camera slowly pulls up and away until the chair is lost among the waste. Overcast light, realistic, cinematic, shallow depth of field."*

To match-cut cleanly, make the AI chair look like yours: describe its colour and shape, or give your photo of it as the reference image.

### Capture tips
- Do Not Disturb on, full battery, notification bar hidden, larger system font, full brightness.
- Before each screen recording, pick the right persona and spoken language: Hindi for my phone, English for Ananya's.
- Record 3 seconds of stillness before and after every take.
- Camera footage is horizontal, 1080p, 30 fps. Phone screen recordings stay vertical; the edit puts them in phone frames.
- Speed up waiting time and label it "sped up". Never fake an app result. Label AI-generated shots. Leave the "Sample data" badges visible.

## Brief for Claude CLI (Remotion)

> Build a 3:00, 1920×1080, 30 fps Remotion video for Kintsugi, our hackathon entry. Follow `../kintsugi/docs/video/script.md` scene by scene, one composition per scene with the durations in its timeline, combined in a `Main` composition. Use the colours and fonts from `../kintsugi/frontend/src/styles.css` (indigo, porcelain, gold). Until real clips arrive in `public/clips/`, use labelled placeholders named after each file in the shot list.
> Scene 1 matters most: build the fast rewind effect (clips 01g → 01f → 01d played backwards, sped up, with `sfx_rewind.wav`), the gold crack that runs across the frozen frame and resolves into the logo and tagline, the source captions, and the "AI-generated" label on AI shots.
> Then: phone screen recordings in a clean, minimal phone frame (scene 5 shows two phones side by side), lower-thirds, the "Live · sped up 2×" badge, the animated architecture diagram in scene 7 with each Gemini model lighting up as the voiceover names it, the gold line crossing out the landfill in scene 8, and the end card with the URL.
> Burn in captions from `public/voiceover.wav` with @remotion/captions and whisper.cpp, and add English subtitles for the Hindi lines. Style: calm and premium, white space, gentle spring animations, no flashy transitions, quiet music under the voice, no music during the chair breaking. Render to `out/kintsugi.mp4`.

### Final checks before upload
- 3:00 or under.
- Watch it once on a phone with sound and once muted: the story must work from the captions alone.
- Show the first 30 seconds to someone who hasn't seen the app. They should be able to say what the problem is and why they want to see what happens next.
- Every number on screen has its source; AI shots are labelled.
- The URL on the end card opens the live app.
