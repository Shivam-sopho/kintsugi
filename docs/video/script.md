# Kintsugi demo video: script and shot list

Length: **3:00 exactly**. Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, English voiceover, burned-in captions (Hindi lines subtitled in English).

The idea: follow **one chair and two people** from the bin to a new home. The first 30 seconds set up a small human problem; the last 15 seconds resolve it. Everything in between is the product making that happen, with the rest of the system shown for a few seconds as proof that it scales.

People (teammates or friends play them on camera; they match the app's personas):
- **Priya** is moving out tomorrow. Her broken teak chair is going in the bin. She speaks Hindi.
- **Ananya** is opening a café 3 km away and is about to buy new chairs. She speaks English.

Voiceover: about 380 words, read slowly (about 130 words a minute). Leave the gaps marked *(natural sound)*, where a person or the app speaks.

## 1. The story (0:00–0:30)

The goal of these 30 seconds: make a judge care about one chair, see that the waste is an information problem rather than a stuff problem, and understand the name before the product appears.

**0:00–0:06 · The name**
- **On screen:** black, then the sound of a ceramic bowl cracking. A broken bowl, its cracks slowly filling with gold. Build this as a Remotion animation: a line-drawn bowl, the crack drawn in gold. Or film a real kintsugi bowl if anyone has one.
- **Voiceover:**
  > In Japan, when a bowl breaks, it isn't thrown away. The cracks are filled with gold, and the bowl becomes worth more than before.

**0:06–0:17 · Two people, cross-cut**
- **On screen:** cut between two real scenes, about 2 seconds each, with quick, natural sound.
  - Priya on moving day: boxes, packing tape, then she drags the broken chair out and leaves it by the bin (`01a_priya_moving.mp4`, `01b_chair_bin.mp4`).
  - Ananya in her half-finished café, laptop open on a furniture site, cursor on "Buy 12 new chairs" (`01c_ananya_cart.mp4`).
  - Small location captions: *"Priya · Indiranagar"* and *"Ananya · Koramangala, 3 km away"*.
- **Voiceover:**
  > Priya is moving out tomorrow. Her old teak chair has a broken leg, so it's going in the bin. Three kilometres away, Ananya is about to buy twelve new chairs for her café.

**0:17–0:24 · The gap**
- **On screen:** a clean animated map of Bengaluru. Two dots light up, Priya's and Ananya's, close together, with nothing between them. Then the frame fills with dots: thousands of homes and building sites, all unconnected. Quick cut to real debris by a roadside (`01d_debris.mp4`). Small text: *"Source: Centre for Science and Environment, 2020"*.
- **Voiceover:**
  > They'll never meet. Multiply that by every home and building site: India recycles about one per cent of its building waste. The problem isn't the stuff. Nobody knows where it is.

**0:24–0:30 · Gold**
- **On screen:** on the map, a gold seam draws itself between Priya's dot and Ananya's, the same gold as the bowl. It resolves into the Kintsugi logo, then the line *"Google Maps for the circular economy."*
- **Voiceover:**
  > Kintsugi fills that gap with gold. It's Google Maps for the circular economy.

## 2. Scout: Priya films the chair (0:30–1:00)

**On screen:**
- Priya, back at the chair, opens Kintsugi. Home screen: *"Moving, renovating or demolishing? Don't throw it out."* (`02a_home.mp4`)
- Over her shoulder: she films the chair and says in Hindi, audible:
  *"इस कुर्सी का एक पैर टूट गया है। लकड़ी सागौन की है।"*
  Subtitle: *"One leg of this chair is broken. The wood is teak."* *(natural sound, about 4 s)* (`02b_film_chair.mp4`)
- Screen recording of the live scan card: steps ticking off, the timer, "Gemini is watching and listening" (`02c_live_scan.mp4`). Badge top right: *"Live · sped up 2×"*.
- The passport: what it becomes, its parts and materials, the price with its sources, the ladder's choice. Kintsugi reads the answer aloud; let 2 seconds of that voice play (`02d_passport.mp4`).
- Lower-third: *"Gemini 2.5 Pro · Gemini 2.5 Flash with Google Search · Gemini Embedding 2"*

**Voiceover:**
> Priya films the chair and just talks, in Hindi. Gemini watches and listens. It lists every part and material, and prices the chair with Google Search, citing its sources. Then code, not the model, picks the best next life. [Read the line that matches the real result on screen:]
> - *If reuse or repair:* A new leg costs far less than a new chair, so it should be used again.
> - *If parts:* Repair would cost more than it's worth, so the teak and the steel go to people who can use them.

(For the payoff to work, the chair should come out as reuse or repair. Pick a chair that's clearly fixable and say so in Hindi: "a leg is broken".)

## 5. The deal room: the hero moment (1:26–2:08)

**On screen:**
- Two real phones side by side on a table, hands visible (`05a_two_phones_wide.mp4`). Left: Priya's phone in Hindi. Right: Ananya's phone in English.
- Screen recordings of both phones during the same live negotiation (`05b_deal_hi.mp4`, `05c_deal_en.mp4`), shown side by side in phone frames.
- Let one Hindi agent line play aloud in the Gemini voice, subtitled *(natural sound, about 5 s)*.
- The guardrail note appears, "limit held". Zoom in on it.
- Ananya's thumb taps Approve. The celebration plays (`05d_approve.mp4`).
- Lower-third: *"A2A protocol · private limits enforced in code"*

**How to get it live:** with Ananya's standing request saved, list Priya's chair. The live market has Ananya's agent open a negotiation within seconds. Record that real negotiation, not the replayed demo.

**Voiceover:**
> Now the hard part: bargaining across languages, without giving away your limit. Priya's agent speaks Hindi. Ananya's agent speaks English. They negotiate with each other over Google's A2A protocol.
> *(natural sound: one Hindi line, subtitled)*
> Each person's limit stays private, and code stops either agent from crossing it. Nothing is sold until a person taps approve. Or switch on Autopilot, and your agent sells within your limit while you get on with your day.

### Capture tips
- Do Not Disturb on, full battery, notification bar hidden, larger system font, full brightness.
- Before each screen recording, pick the right persona in the profile and set the spoken language: Hindi for Priya, English for Ananya.
- Record 3 seconds of stillness before and after every take.
- Camera footage is horizontal, 1080p, 30 fps. Phone screen recordings stay vertical; the edit puts them in phone frames.
- Speed up waiting time and label it "sped up". Never fake a result. Leave the "Sample data" badges visible.

## Brief for building the video with Claude CLI and Remotion

Paste into Claude CLI inside the Remotion project:

> Build a 3:00, 1920×1080, 30 fps Remotion video for Kintsugi, our hackathon entry. Follow `../kintsugi/docs/video/script.md` scene by scene, one composition per scene with the exact durations in its timeline, combined in a `Main` composition. Use the colours and fonts from `../kintsugi/frontend/src/styles.css` (indigo, porcelain, gold). Until real clips arrive in `public/clips/`, use labelled placeholders named after each file in the shot list.
> Build these first, because the opening depends on them: (1) a line-drawn bowl whose crack fills with gold, synced to `sfx_bowl_crack.wav`; (2) a minimal animated Bengaluru map where two dots light up, the frame fills with unconnected dots, and a gold seam draws between the two and resolves into the logo; (3) the same seam glowing again in the final scene.
> Then: phone screen recordings inside a clean, minimal phone frame (scene 5 shows two phones side by side), lower-thirds, the "Live · sped up 2×" badge, location captions, source captions, the animated architecture diagram in scene 7 with each Gemini model lighting up as the voiceover names it, and the end card with the URL.
> Burn in captions from `public/voiceover.wav` with @remotion/captions and whisper.cpp, and add English subtitles for the Hindi lines. Style: calm and premium, lots of white space, gentle spring animations, no flashy transitions, quiet music under the voice. Render to `out/kintsugi.mp4`.

### Final checks before upload
- 3:00 or under.
- Watch it once on a phone with sound and once muted: the story must work from the captions alone.
- Ask someone who hasn't seen the app to watch the first 30 seconds. They should be able to tell you who Priya and Ananya are and what the problem is.
- Every number on screen has its source.
- The URL on the end card opens the live app.
